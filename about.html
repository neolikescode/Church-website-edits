Our mission

To lead people into the discovery, recovery, and fulfillment of their original divine destinies through the uncompromised preaching of the Word of Faith and the demonstration of the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our vision

To raise and transform communities into a covenant family of believers who, through the discipline of the Word and the walk of faith, rediscover their God-ordained destinies and manifest His divine purpose on earth.

Our divine mandate

Our divine mandate is rooted in Exodus 25:40:

“See that you make them according to the pattern shown you on the mountain.” (NIV)

Read Exodus 25:40 on BibleGateway (opens in a new tab)

  • To establish lives upon precept upon precept and line upon line through sound biblical teaching.
  • To guide people into the discovery of their original purpose, divine identity, and kingdom assignment.
  • To reveal and manifest the nature, power, and glory of God on the earth.
